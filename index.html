🚀 Quick Start Guide

Get the most out of OscarAnalytics in 4 steps

1

Understanding your Dashboard

Your dashboard shows 5 key metrics: Revenue, Orders, Sessions, Conversion Rate, and Avg. Order Value. Each has a trend arrow showing change vs. last period.

2

Reading the Checkout Funnel

The funnel shows where customers drop off during purchase. Click the red drop-off arrows to see which users left and from which region/browser.

3

Setting up Alerts

Go to Settings → Email Alerts to get notified when KPIs change by more than 10%. Check Notifications for real-time alerts on stock, orders, and search gaps.

4

Exporting Reports

Visit Export / Reports to download weekly PDFs, monthly summaries, or raw CSV data. Schedule auto-delivery so reports arrive in your inbox every Monday.

📖 Glossary of Terms

What each metric means in plain language

The percentage of visitors who make a purchase. If 100 people visit your store and 3 buy something, your conversion rate is 3%. A healthy rate for Kenyan e-commerce is 2–5%.
The percentage of visitors who leave after viewing just one page. A high bounce rate (above 50%) suggests your landing page isn't engaging enough or loads too slowly.
The total amount a customer spends on your store over their entire relationship with you. A CLV of KES 18,000 means each customer is worth KES 18,000 in total revenue across all their orders.
The average amount spent per order. Calculated as total revenue divided by total orders. Increasing AOV (e.g., through upsells or bundles) is often easier than increasing traffic.
When a customer leaves the purchase process before completing it. Each funnel stage (View → Cart → Checkout → Payment → Order) has a drop-off rate showing where customers abandon.
